Found while fixing F6 (pre-existing race in post_entry):
test_concurrent_imports_book_the_bundle_once failed in 2 of 8 repeated runs with
DuplicateProviderEventError("... resource_id='INV2-4RUV-XPEH-4F3C-T8BW') already has a
invoice_issued entry") instead of returning DUPLICATE. ON CONFLICT targeted only the
idempotency key, so a concurrent replay could hit the provider-event unique index first.
No double posting occurred (the index refused it), but replays were not reliably
idempotent. After the fix (every unique index is an arbiter): 15 of 15 repeated runs of
the three concurrency tests passed.
